SummerSlam 2026 Key Moments

The major outcomes from WWE's two-night event in Minneapolis

Oba Femi Defeats The Beast

Oba Femi beat Brock Lesnar inside Hell in a Cell. After the match, Lesnar embraced Femi and called him the future of WWE.

Randy Orton Returns

Randy Orton delivered an RKO to Cody Rhodes, helping CM Punk retain the Undisputed WWE Championship.

Kevin Owens Returns

Kevin Owens returned after 17 months and won a Fatal 4-Way to become No. 1 Contender to the Undisputed WWE Championship.

Three New Champions

Baron Corbin, Chelsea Green and Chad Gable left Minneapolis with championship gold.

Roman Reigns Retains

Roman Reigns defeated Seth Rollins in the Night 2 main event, followed by a Shield fist bump between the longtime rivals.
