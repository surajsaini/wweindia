How the Elimination Chamber Match Works

The established match format; WWE has not yet confirmed how it will be used in 2027.

Six Competitors

6 Superstars

Two competitors begin in the ring while four wait inside individual transparent pods.

Timed Entrants

Pods Open

At timed intervals, one pod opens and another Superstar enters the match.

Eliminations

Pin or Submit

A competitor leaves the match after being pinned or submitting. The last remaining Superstar wins.
